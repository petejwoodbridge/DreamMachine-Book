## Editor's Pick: Can AI write better stories?

A new study has found that readers often rated AI generated short stories as higher quality and more engaging than human written ones.... and frequently couldn't tell which was which.

There's an interesting nuance here: the study isn't saying AI is *objectively* better at creativity. It highlights how strongly audiences respond to the finished work, regardless of who, or what, created it.

That raises an increasingly important question for the creative industries: if audiences can't reliably distinguish AI generated work from human work... and in some cases actually prefer it, as we've also seen in previous studies around AI-generated music... what does that mean for how we evaluate creativity, quality and authorship?

Read more- [https://www.theguardian.com/technology/2026/aug/05/ai-generated-stories-rated-better-quality-than-human-written-ones-study-finds](https://www.theguardian.com/technology/2026/aug/05/ai-generated-stories-rated-better-quality-than-human-written-ones-study-finds)
