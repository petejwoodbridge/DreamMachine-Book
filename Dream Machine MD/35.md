## Editor's Pick: Unreal Engine's AI Future — From Tools to Creative Partners

Epic Games announced part of its approach for how AI will reshape game development this week — not just by adding AI features, but by embedding AI directly into creative workflows.

Announced at the State of Unreal, recent developments include **Unreal MCP** (Model Context Protocol), **LLM-powered tools** and video model integrations, point towards a future where creators can direct the engine through natural language, automate complex tasks and accelerate production workflows.

The goal is simple: reduce time spent on repetitive work — building assets, setting up systems, creating variations and debugging — so creators can focus more on ideas and storytelling.

The shift has sparked debate, with some developers concerned AI could replace established workflows or reduce the craft behind tools like Blueprints. But Epic's bigger opportunity may not be replacing developers — **it may be expanding who can become one.**

By lowering technical barriers, Unreal could enable more creators to build interactive worlds and utilise the epic ecosystem across games, film and immersive. For Epic, AI is not just a productivity tool play — **it could be a way to grow the entire creator ecosystem.**
