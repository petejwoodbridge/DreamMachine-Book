## Editors Pick: Disney & The AI you build on your own knowledge

*[Image: Disney's JARVIS]*

One of the more interesting AI stories this week isn't a new model.

It's **Disney's J.A.R.V.I.S.**

Disney has reportedly rolled out its internal AI system to more than **2,000 Imagineers**, giving them access to decades of accumulated creative knowledge, processes and expertise.

The real opportunity is taking everything an organisation already knows — its **data, archives, intellectual property, workflows and institutional knowledge** — and making it accessible, searchable and useful in completely new ways.

For creative organisations, that will become a serious competitive advantage.

This is exactly the kind of work we do at [DreamLab – AI](https://uk.linkedin.com/company/dreamlab-ai-consulting) and [DreamLab](https://dreamlab.org.uk/) — helping organisations, institutions and individuals **understand their own data, knowledge and processes, how to protect it, and how to turn it into AI powered advantage.**

We even call the system **JARVIS**....... but we probably shouldn't tell Disney that. 😅 🐭

## Stats of the week...

Luminate found a **48 percentage-point gap** in attitudes towards AI-generated music between 13–17-year-olds (+19% net interest) and 18–24-year-olds (-29%). [LINK](https://musically.com/2026/07/27/luminate-study-explores-americans-interest-in-genai-content/)

Meanwhile, **more than 1 billion people now use Gemini every month** — with users increasingly talking to it, showing it things, uploading work, generating images and asking it to take actions. [LINK](https://9to5google.com/2026/08/11/gemini-app-1-billion/)

- It is the company's 14th product to hit that milestone along with Search, Gmail, Android, Maps, Chrome, Play, and YouTube.
- 63% are talking to it.
- 1 in 5 Gemini Live interactions involve a camera or screen.
- 38% of school-related requests include an attachment.
- 150M+ images are generated every day.
- And Gemini can now take actions across 40+ apps.

And in games...... **52% of game developers now say generative AI is having a negative impact on the industry.** That's up from **30% last year** and just **18% the year before.**. [LINK](https://gdconf.com/article/gdc-2026-state-of-the-game-industry-reveals-impact-of-layoffs-generative-ai-and-more/)

And yet AI adoption continues to grow behind the scenes.
