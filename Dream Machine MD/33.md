## Editor's Pick: Dream Machine – A Field Guide to the New Creative Economy

*Dream Machine - AI and the New Creative Economy*

Over the past eight months, we've watched and monitored AI move **from experimentation to implementation** across film, music, games, publishing, advertising and media. The debate has shifted from *whether* these tools matter to *how* they will reshape creative careers, industries and institutions. Alongside that transition, this newsletter has tracked the developments, controversies, breakthroughs and emerging patterns that have defined the conversation. **That work has now become a book.**

*Dream Machine: Artificial Intelligence and the Next Creative Economy* brings together the research, analysis and frameworks developed across thirty-two editions of this newsletter into a single living volume. Spanning more than 500 pages, it examines everything from **copyright battles and synthetic performers to AI-assisted production, new creative roles, authenticity, labour market shifts, emerging business models and the future relationship between human creativity and machine capability.**

What makes this book different is its perspective. It is written not from the outside looking in, but from inside the transition itself—by a group working across creative technology, innovation, production and research while these changes are actively unfolding. Rather than treating AI as either salvation or catastrophe, the book attempts to hold the whole picture at once: **the opportunities, the risks, the contradictions and the choices that will define the next decade of creative work.**

As technical execution becomes increasingly accessible through AI systems, human value shifts towards intent, taste, judgement, originality, community and meaning. The question is no longer what machines can make. The question is what humans choose to create with them- as always.

Whether you're a filmmaker, musician, game developer, designer, producer, educator, policymaker or simply trying to understand where creative work might be heading next, *Dream Machine* offers one of the most comprehensive maps yet of the terrain happening now and beyond.
